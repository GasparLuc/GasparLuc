

## Hi, I'm Gaspar

I'm a developer based in Argentina, focused on building online presence for companies and entrepreneurs: websites, automations, advertising campaigns, and everything in between.

I work at the intersection of the technical and the commercial. On one side, I build custom websites and internal dashboards for clients who need something more specific than any off-the-shelf template can offer. On the other, I manage SEO strategies and Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns aimed at driving measurable results, not empty impressions.

On the development side, I work mainly with Python and JavaScript. I use n8n to orchestrate complex automations, Puppeteer and Playwright for web scraping, and Docker on a self-hosted VPS to deploy services. I'm particularly interested in projects that require integrating multiple tools and APIs into coherent workflows.

I also build Telegram bots and alerting systems for internal processes, whenever the goal is to reduce repetitive manual work.

Feel free to reach out or take a look at my repositories.
